Rule 1

Be in the sources they read.

The Google profile, the major directories and the roundup articles already ranking. That is the material being quoted.

Rule 2

Answer in the first 40 words.

Models lift the part that answers the question directly. Bury it and you lose.

Rule 3

Make the entity impossible to doubt.

One name, one address, one phone, everywhere. A model that finds conflicting details tends to leave the business out rather than guess.
